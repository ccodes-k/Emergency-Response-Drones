# Emergency-Response-Drones

This repository investigates the deployment of autonomous unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) operating as cooperative swarms to enhance real-time disaster response capabilities. By integrating a decentralized swarm intelligence algorithm—adapted from bio-inspired models such as Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) and Ant Colony Optimization (ACO)—the proposed system enables drones to self-organize, distribute tasks dynamically, and maintain robust communication in uncertain and GPS-denied environments. The study presents a simulation evaluation of swarm-based fire detection, mapping, and suppression workflows, comparing them against conventional single-drone and centrally controlled multi-UAV methods. Results indicate that swarming algorithms significantly reduce response latency, improve area coverage efficiency by up to 40%, and increase system resilience against individual drone failure. The findings demonstrate the feasibility of swarm-enabled UAV systems as a scalable and cost-effective asset for first responders, with implications for future integration into smart-city emergency management infrastructures.
